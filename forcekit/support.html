Support

ForceKIT is free. No catch.

There's no premium tier, no paywalled features, no "pro" upsell waiting around the corner. If ForceKIT saves you time on a Tuesday afternoon debugging session, you can say thanks with a coffee — entirely optional, entirely appreciated.

Support the project

ForceKIT is built and maintained independently, on the side, by one person who spends too much time in Salesforce dev orgs. Every bit of support goes straight back into the extension — no investors, no team to fund, just time.
