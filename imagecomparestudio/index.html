Features

01

Precise manual alignment

Drag to move, scroll to zoom in 15% steps, fine rotation controls, and horizontal/vertical flip — everything you need to line up two photos exactly.

02

Opacity overlay while aligning

Blend the after image over the before image at any opacity while you align, so you can see both at once.

03

Grid, guides & snapping

A toggleable grid overlay, center guides, and snap-to-center make precise, repeatable positioning effortless.

04

Draggable comparison slider

Reveal the after image with a smooth horizontal or vertical slider — keyboard-accessible, with an animated handle.

05

Side-by-side & onion skin

Switch to a side-by-side view, or blend both images together in onion-skin mode, whenever a slider isn't what you need.

06

Pixel-inspection magnifier

Zoom into individual pixels with a built-in magnifier lens to check fine detail and alignment accuracy.

07

Full-resolution export

Export as PNG or JPEG at full resolution (up to 10000×10000px), or as a standalone interactive HTML page you can share or embed anywhere.

08

Save & reopen projects

Every comparison — images, alignment, and viewer settings — can be saved as a project and reopened later, with autosave built in.

09

Private by design

No server, no account, no tracking. Every image and every setting stays on your device — the extension has no network permissions at all.
