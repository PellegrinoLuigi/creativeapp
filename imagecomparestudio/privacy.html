Privacy Policy

What Image Compare Studio does with your data — in plain terms.

Image Compare Studio is a Chrome extension for aligning and comparing before/after photos. It has no server of its own: every image, every project, and every setting stays inside your browser, on your device.

Last updated 8 August 2026

Summary

No server, no account, no tracking. Image Compare Studio doesn't collect, store, or transmit your data to us or anyone else. The extension requests no network permissions at all — it is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere.

Your images

The before/after images you load are processed entirely in memory, inside your browser tab, to align and render the comparison. They are never uploaded, never sent to any server, and never seen by anyone but you.

What is stored locally

If you choose to save a project, Image Compare Studio stores it — the images, their alignment, and the comparison settings — in IndexedDB, a database built into your browser. App preferences (theme, language, grid, autosave, export quality) are saved separately via chrome.storage.local. Both live only on your device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No ads, and no data ever sold or shared with anyone.
  • No third-party servers in the loop — there is no backend at all.
  • No account, sign-in, or personal information requested or required.

Permissions, explained

PermissionWhat it's for
storageSave your preferences (theme, language, grid, autosave, export quality) locally via chrome.storage.local — small key/value data only.
unlimitedStorageSave projects (images, alignment, and settings) locally in IndexedDB. Images can be large (up to 10000×10000px) and several projects can be saved at once, so the extension requests this to avoid hitting the browser's default storage quota.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project from the sidebar removes it immediately. Uninstalling the extension removes everything else — all saved projects and preferences — since nothing exists outside your browser's local storage.

Changes to this policy

If what Image Compare Studio collects or does ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will reflect it.
