Why local-first

The same three promises, in every extension.

Local-first

Everything runs inside your browser. Your files, data and settings stay on your device — there is no CreativeApp server to send them to.

Free, for real

No premium tiers, no paywalled features, no upsells. Every extension is complete and free on the Chrome Web Store.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no accounts. The extensions only talk to the services you point them at, like your own Salesforce org.

Like these tools?

They're built and maintained in spare time by one developer. A coffee keeps them free and growing.
