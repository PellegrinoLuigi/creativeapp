What's inside

01

Calculator & productivity

A safe expression calculator (no eval) with history and a scientific mode, plus Timer, Stopwatch, Notes and a Clipboard Manager.

02

JSON toolkit

Format, minify and validate — and repair broken JSON: missing quotes and commas, trailing commas, comments, single quotes.

03

Encoders & decoders

Base64 with file import/export, URL encoding, a local JWT decoder, UUID v4 generator and a Unix timestamp converter.

04

Regex tester

Live match highlighting and one-click templates for email, URL, IPv4, UUID, dates and more.

05

Text tools

Word and character counts, case conversion, deduplicate and sort lines, Lorem Ipsum by paragraph or exact character count.

06

Keyboard symbols

Click to copy ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ → — … € ± ≠ and 150+ more special characters and Greek letters.

07

Converters

Length, weight, temperature, data size, time, speed and area — all computed offline.

08

QR & web

QR codes for text, URLs and Wi-Fi; URL parser; page information; HTTP header viewer.

09

Private by design

Everything runs and is stored locally. The only network request is the one you trigger yourself in the HTTP Header Viewer. Light / dark themes, 6 languages, Manifest V3.

Privacy

Your data stays with you

OmniKit has no server of its own — every tool runs inside your browser, and everything you create stays on your device.
