Privacy Policy

What Profile Manager does with your data — in plain terms.

Profile Manager is a Chrome extension for Salesforce admins who need to view, compare, and edit Profile permissions. It has no server of its own: everything happens inside your browser, either on local files you pick or against the Salesforce org you're already logged into.
